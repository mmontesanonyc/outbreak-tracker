About this outbreak tracker

This animated outbreak tracker runs on dummy data. It charts the daily number of cases, and the cumulative number of cases by geography. Animating over time is a nice way to show change.

On the map, the bubble size is proportional to the total number of cases for that area; and the opacity is proportional to the recency of the cases - the paler the bubble, the further in the past the most recent case is.

To customize this, a few simple configurations - color, chart and map dimension, playback speed, and the central points of your areas that you are mapping - are stored in config.js.

There is extremely minimal CSS, so this is ready to adopt your own styling.
